Privacy Policy

How Bank Vault handles your information.

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Bank Vault collects, uses, stores, and manages information when you use the app.

Information We Collect

When you create and use a Bank Vault account, we may collect information such as your name, email address, account identifier, and information that you choose to save in the app.

Information You Save

Bank Vault allows you to organize financial and other account-related information into records. Only information that you choose to enter or upload is stored as part of your records.

How We Use Your Information

Information is used to provide Bank Vault features, authenticate your account, display your saved records, maintain the service, and improve the app.

Cloud Storage

Bank Vault uses Supabase to provide account authentication and cloud database services. Information stored in your account may therefore be processed and stored through Supabase's infrastructure.

Access to Records

Your records are associated with your account. Authorized Bank Vault administrators may have access to stored records when necessary to operate, maintain, troubleshoot, or administer the service.

Information Sharing

Bank Vault does not use your information for purposes unrelated to providing and operating the service. Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary for authentication, storage, hosting, notifications, and other app functions.

Account Security

You are responsible for keeping your account credentials and Master Password private. Do not share passwords, authentication codes, or other account credentials with anyone.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, Bank Vault may use push notification services to send account or application notifications to your device.

Your Choices

You can manage the information stored in your account through the app. You can also sign out of your account. If you want your account or stored information removed, contact the person or organization responsible for operating Bank Vault.

Children's Privacy

Bank Vault is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate permission and supervision.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Bank Vault's features, data practices, or services change. The updated version will be displayed on this screen.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, contact the person or organization operating Bank Vault.
